# Implementation Summary — TS-Roster Management (My Team(s) Tab)
Sources: input/requirements/TS-Roster Management (My Team(s) Tab)-161026-092450.pdf (cited as TS-Roster Management.pdf p.N; 22 pages). Cross-checked against input/requirements/TS-Store Home (Home Tab)-161026-011919.pdf (cited as TS-Store Home.pdf) for conflicts only.

## 1. Scope
- In scope: the My Team(s) tab in the Team Store workspace. It covers divisions/teams structure, rosters and player records, the duplicate-number policy, the Roster Bank surface, product visibility and assignment (League only), and moving players between teams (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.2, p.7-18)
- Store types: **League (Multi-Team Hub)**, with divisions + teams + canvas, and **Single-Team Locker**, with no division tier or grouping UI. On Single-Team, the tab is labelled "My Team" and opens straight into the team's player table (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3, p.7)
- The structure editor is a single shared component with two hosts: this tab, and League store creation Step 2 (Divisions & Teams) (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.20-21)
- A roster is **never** required to launch, and a team may have no roster (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.2, p.6)
- Out of scope / owned elsewhere:
  - Roster Bank capture, pre-grouping and commit mechanics (Roster-Optional Architecture spec, Epic C)
  - Order snapshot model and pending state (Order Management, Epic B)
  - Catalog record, pricing and team-code bindings (Products tab spec)
  - Manager records and fulfillment mapping definition (Store General Settings, Epic A3)
  - Storefront team selector (Roster-Optional Architecture, Epic D)
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.14-15, p.20-22)
- Explicitly not built: a bulk "sync roster to orders" control (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11); a Home attention card for divergence (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11); rep-facing or shopper-facing team-code entry (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.21)

## 2. Actors & Roles
- **Store managers:** reps, team managers and league directors (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.2)
- **Rep:** all actions, including structure, duplicate policy, Roster Bank commits, product assignment, editing players who have already ordered, and pending-order update (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.6-7)
- **Team manager:** add, edit, import, remove, clear and delete per the user stories. Receives the emailed roster template (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.6-7, p.9) ⚠ open question OI-24 (no permission matrix or scope)
- **Parent/player (shopper):** picks a jersey number on the storefront PDP and at checkout, and supplies a shipping address at checkout (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12-13)
- **Product assignment permissions** follow the Products tab's existing rules. This surface adds no new authority (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.21)

## 3. Business Rules
**Object model**
- BR-1: Entities are Division (League only, optional), Team (all stores, name required), Roster (optional) and Player record (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3)
- BR-2: A roster attaches to exactly one team, and a team holds at most one roster. There are no free-standing rosters (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3, p.20)
- BR-3: A team sits inside a division or stands alone under the league (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3, p.7)
- BR-4: Deleting a division keeps its teams. They become standalone, drop the division's branding, and keep their rosters and players intact (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.20) ⚠ open question OI-7
- BR-5: Deleting a team removes the team and any roster it holds (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10, p.20) ⚠ open question OI-11
- BR-6: Every division and team has a deterministic, human-readable team code (e.g. RT47A). Codes are generated by the backend, stamped onto orders, and read-only in the workspace. They are never entered by the rep or the shopper and are hidden during store creation (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.21) ⚠ open question OI-6

**Player records**
- BR-7: Only First Name and Last Name are required. A record with no contact channel is valid (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3, p.6, p.22)
- BR-8: Contact fields are format-validated only when supplied (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3, p.9, p.22)
- BR-9: Records with no contact channel: Share Team Store Link is copy-only for that player, and Send Reminder is disabled with a reason stated (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.6, p.10, p.22) ⚠ open question OI-14
- BR-10: Name on Jersey is converted to UPPERCASE dynamically (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.4) ⚠ open question OI-4

**Order snapshot and divergence (governing rule)**
- BR-11: Roster edits are never blocked by order state. Jersey Number and Name on Jersey edits on a player who has ordered save with no block, no modal and no warning (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.2, p.11)
- BR-12: A roster edit never mutates a placed order. Personalization (number, name on jersey, size, shipping address) is snapshotted on the order line at purchase and never re-read from the roster (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.2, p.11, p.20) ⚠ open question OI-2
- BR-13: Divergence is a derived comparison of current roster values against each order line's snapshot, not a stored flag. It resolves itself when either side changes (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.20)
- BR-14: Divergence is informational only. It never blocks submission, never affects the launch gate, creates no Home card, and does not change team-gear completion (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11)
- BR-15: While the order is pending (pre-submission, Order Management Epic B), the divergence line offers one per-line "update the order to #N" action that rewrites that line's snapshot from current roster values. There is no bulk variant, and it is refused server-side once the order is submitted (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11, p.20)
- BR-16: After submission, the divergence line shows with no action (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11)

**Direct-to-Home address**
- BR-17: The Player Shipping Address field and column exist only when fulfillment is Direct to Home Delivery (set in Step 1 — Basics). The field is optional for the rep (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.5, p.11-12)
- BR-18: Write-back fills an existing record's blank address from checkout. If no matching record exists, a Roster Bank capture is created instead. The two must never both fire for one order (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12) ⚠ open question OI-16
- BR-19: Write-back is one-way. Editing an address on the roster never changes a placed order (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12) ⚠ open question OI-15 ⚠ open question OI-1

**Duplicate-number policy**
- BR-20: Each roster owns its own Allow Duplicate Numbers setting, and validation always reads the roster's own value (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12, p.21)
- BR-21: The league-level control (Settings → Team & Roster, Roster Rules card, also written by Step 1 — Basics Epic D1) is a bulk write that cascades through divisions to every roster. It is never inherited at read time and never overrides a roster afterward (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12-13, p.21)
- BR-22: New rosters copy the league-level default at creation only, including rosters created in League creation Step 2 (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13, p.21) ⚠ open question OI-17
- BR-23: Mixed state: when rosters disagree with the league default, the Settings control shows "mixed", and each roster view states the policy in force (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13)
- BR-24: When duplicates are off, the taken-number set is committed player numbers ∪ pending Roster Bank captures for that team. It excludes the edited player's own number (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13, p.21-22)
- BR-25: Taken numbers render disabled and unselectable on every entry surface: the add row, edit drawer, storefront PDP and checkout (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13, p.22) ⚠ open question OI-3
- BR-26: The inline error "Number [X] is already assigned to [Player Name]" is a backstop for CSV import and direct field entry only, and is enforced server-side (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13, p.22)
- BR-27: When duplicates are on, there is no constraint and no error, and captures hold no numbers. An in-use number is labelled "duplicate", not "taken" (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13-14)
- BR-28: Numbers are released immediately on player delete, bulk delete, Clear Roster and move. A capture's number is released when the capture is dismissed from the Bank (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10, p.13, p.17, p.22)
- BR-29: The taken-number set is invalidated on player delete, bulk delete, Clear Roster, capture commit and capture dismissal (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.22)
- BR-30: Turning duplicates off is never blocked. No validation sweep runs and no records change; existing shared numbers are grandfathered (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.14, p.22)
- BR-31: A grandfathered number counts as taken, so no third player can join it (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.14)
- BR-32: The grandfathered-duplicate report is a derived per-roster query: numbers held by more than one committed record while the policy is off. It shows "duplicates off · N players share #X, assigned before the rule changed", with the affected rows identifiable. It clears itself when a player is deleted, moved or renumbered, or when the policy is turned back on. It never gates anything (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.14, p.22)

**Roster lifecycle**
- BR-33: Clear Roster removes all committed players and releases their numbers, but keeps the roster entity attached to its team. It is disabled when the roster is empty (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10, p.20)
- BR-34: Delete Roster removes the roster entity and the team survives with no roster. It is not offered on a Single-Team store's only roster (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10, p.20) ⚠ open question OI-13
- BR-35: Clear and Delete Roster never touch pending Roster Bank captures or product assignments (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10, p.20)
- BR-36: Moving the last player off a roster leaves the roster in place (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.18)

**Roster Bank**
- BR-37: Committing a capture writes an ordinary player record at the values captured at checkout. If the team has no roster, the first commit creates one (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.15)
- BR-38: Captures are not players: they are excluded from the player table and player counts, and unaffected by Clear/Delete Roster. They do hold jersey numbers and do count toward team-gear completion (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.15) ⚠ open question OI-18
- BR-39: The Roster Bank is not surfaced during store creation (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.15)

**Product assignment (League only)**
- BR-40: The Products tab and this tab read and write the same assignments (team-code bindings), and neither is authoritative (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.15, p.21)
- BR-41: Assignment scope is modelled at two levels, team and division. Division-wide assignments show on teams as "via division" rows (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.16-17, p.21)
- BR-42: A division-wide assignment applies to teams added to the division later, with no re-assignment step (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.17) ⚠ open question OI-20
- BR-43: Product assignment belongs to the team, not the player. A moved player inherits the destination team's products and loses products assigned only to the origin team (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10, p.18)

**Move player**
- BR-44: A move releases the player's number on the origin roster and makes it selectable there immediately (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.17)
- BR-45: If the number is taken or disallowed at the destination (committed records + pending captures), the rep must pick another number or move the player without one (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.18)
- BR-46: Players with team-gear orders cannot be moved. The control is disabled and the reason stated. In a bulk move, blocked players stay on the origin roster and are named in the result (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.18) ⚠ open question OI-21

**Fulfillment mapping**
- BR-47: Each division routes orders to the league manager, a division manager, or individual team managers. The mapping is the same state whether edited in Settings → Distribution & Fulfillment or on the division/team detail (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.21) ⚠ open question OI-9
- BR-48: Unassigned managers gate pending-order submission (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8) ⚠ open question OI-10

## 4. Data
- **Division:** name, brand color, sport (branding), team code, fulfillment mode (league / division / individual teams), division-scoped product assignments (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3, p.8, p.21) ⚠ open question OI-5
- **Team:** name (required), optional division, team code, at most one roster, team-scoped product assignments, effective manager (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3, p.8)
- **Roster:** team (1:1), Allow Duplicate Numbers (bool, per roster). A name is implied by the toast copy (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.3, p.10, p.12) ⚠ open question OI-13
- **Player record** (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.4-6, p.22):
  - First Name: required, string, max 50
  - Last Name: required, string, max 50
  - Jersey Number: optional, integer 00–99, 2-digit numeric input ⚠ open question OI-3
  - Name on Jersey: optional, string, max 15, uppercased, snapshotted at purchase
  - Contact Email: optional, valid email when present
  - Contact Phone Number: optional, 10-digit numeric when present ⚠ open question OI-4
  - Player Shipping Address: optional, street/city/state/ZIP, validated against a backend map service. Direct-to-Home stores only
  - Associated Emails: optional, list of valid emails
  - Size: snapshotted per p.20 but missing from the schema ⚠ open question OI-2
- **Store-level field:** Allow Duplicate Numbers default, a single field written by Step 1 — Basics and by Settings (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13, p.21)
- **Manager records** (owned by Settings): name, email, 10-digit phone, address (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.21)
- **Derived data (not stored):** divergence per order line, grandfathered duplicates, taken-number set, pending-capture counts per team/division/store, per-team and per-division product aggregates (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.20-22)
- **Sources of truth:** structure is shared platform state read by Products, Orders (shipping containers) and Settings (manager mapping). The order line snapshot is the truth for what gets manufactured (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.7, p.20)

## 5. Flows
- **League home:** stats header (divisions · teams · players + pending-capture count), canvas of division headers and team nodes, standalone-teams list, and New Team / New Division actions (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.7, p.14)
- **Structure CRUD:**
  - Create a division (name + brand color) and edit it in a modal, which includes the fulfillment-manager mode
  - Add a team to a division or standalone; rename it; delete it with confirmation
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8) ⚠ open question OI-8
- **Drill-in:** opening a division lists its teams. Opening a team shows breadcrumbs, a branded banner (division color, team avatar) and the player table. The workspace detail panel has Setup and Products tabs (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.16) ⚠ open question OI-26
- **Creation host (League Step 2):** same editor with no branding, no team code, no Epic 6 affordances and no Roster Bank; Setup content only. Manual and CSV entry are available. Structure is committed atomically with the store on deploy (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.15, p.20-21)
- **Add player:** an inline "Add Player" row at the foot of the table contains every field (address only on Direct-to-Home). Confirming with the green check adds the row instantly (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- **CSV import:**
  - Upload Roster button in the toolbar opens a modal with a drag-drop / browse zone and a "Download CSV template" link
  - Rows are validated per row and cell. Invalid rows are skipped and valid rows import. A preview shows valid/skipped counts before confirm
  - Only missing required fields or malformed present values skip a row
  - A duplicate number is an error only on rosters that disallow duplicates
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9) ⚠ open question OI-12
- **Share Roster Template:** emails a blank prolook_roster_template.csv to team managers. The message is pre-drafted and signed by the logged-in rep, and recipients come from Settings manager records (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- **Edit:** row 3-dot menu → Edit opens a pre-populated side drawer. The number control follows the roster's policy, and the player's own number stays selectable (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- **Delete:**
  - Single delete from the row menu happens instantly, with no confirmation
  - Bulk delete uses checkboxes and the selection bar, with a confirmation modal
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10) ⚠ open question OI-11
- **Clear Roster:** a confirmation modal states the committed-player count, that the roster is kept, and whether captures are pending. Success toast: "Roster cleared — n players removed" (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- **Delete Roster:** a confirmation modal states the roster and its n players will be deleted. On confirm the view returns to the team detail. Toast: 'Roster "name" deleted' (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- **Share Team Store Link:**
  - The row menu copies a per-player invite link
  - Multi-select opens a bulk modal with recipients prefilled from contact + associated emails
  - Players with no email are listed as copy-only. If no selected player has an email, the modal offers manual distribution instead of sending
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- **Team Gear column:** shows "n/m team gear items" and opens a gear drawer (ordered vs missing required gear, Send Reminder). Divergence lines appear here, e.g. "ordered as #12 · roster says #7" (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10-11)
- **Sorting:** by Name (A–Z / Z–A) or Number (low→high / high→low), with a live player count (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- **Address entry:**
  - Shows "Verifying address with map service…" while checking
  - On failure, offers retry or leave blank
  - Addresses filled from checkout show a "From checkout" badge; blank addresses show "Awaiting checkout"
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12)
- **Roster Bank:** two entry points to one queue. The per-team view sits on team detail; the store-wide view sits on My Team(s) home, with a team column and filters (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.14)
- **Product canvas (League):**
  - Each team node has a thumbnail strip, an amber "No products yet" state when it has none, and a + that opens the drawer scoped to that team
  - Each division header has a product count pill
  - Hovering a thumbnail shows the shared Products-tab card. Clicking a thumbnail or panel row title opens the full product detail in a modal; edits save to the catalog and refresh in place
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.15-16) ⚠ open question OI-19
- **Team Products tab:** lists assigned products, with inherited ones marked "via division". Has an Add Products split menu (Add New Product / Add from Catalog) and a Roster button (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.16)
- **Division Products tab:** division-wide list plus a per-team coverage table, with an amber "+ Assign products" action on teams without coverage. Has Add Products, Add Team, and "+ Add Product" in the header (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.16)
- **Catalog drawer:**
  - Docks on the right; the rep never leaves the structure view
  - Search plus filters All / Unassigned / Not on [X]
  - Click Assign assigns to the launching scope. Drag onto any team node assigns to that team; drag onto a division header assigns division-wide
  - The UI updates optimistically and reconciles with the server
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.16-17, p.21)
- **Move to team:**
  - Single (row menu) or bulk (selection mode) opens a destination picker listing every other team. On League stores it is grouped by division and searchable
  - On confirm, players leave the origin roster and join the destination
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.17-18) ⚠ open question OI-22 ⚠ open question OI-23

## 6. Integrations
- **Order Management:** order-line snapshot, pending/submitted state, the per-line snapshot rewrite (enforced server-side), the roster↔order join for Team Gear and the gear drawer, and Send Reminder (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10-11, p.20, p.22)
- **Roster-Optional Architecture:** Roster Bank captures (storage, pre-grouping, commit, dismiss) and the storefront team selector (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.14, p.22)
- **Storefront:** PDP and checkout consume the per-roster taken-number set; checkout writes addresses back (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12-13, p.22)
- **Products tab:** shared catalog ↔ team assignment join (read team→products and product→teams), shared product card and detail-page components, and the existing add-product flow (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.15-16, p.21)
- **Settings:**
  - Distribution & Fulfillment manager mapping, with two-way shared state
  - Team & Roster → Roster Rules card (league duplicate default, mixed-state read)
  - Manager records feeding template email recipients
  - (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8-9, p.12, p.21)
- **Store creation:** Step 1 — Basics (fulfillment model, duplicate default) and Step 2 — Divisions & Teams (shared editor) (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.13, p.20-21)
- **Map service** for backend address verification, shared with the freight gate (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.5, p.12, p.21)
- **Email channel:** template send, store-link sends, and per-player invite-link generation (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.22) ⚠ open question OI-14
- **Team database:** "genuine team-database integration: roster sync and member/role management" (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.20)
- **Store Home** reads the stats-header counts (divisions · teams · players, pending captures), the amber no-products state and the "Not assigned" state. Its deep links need scoped routes into this tab (TS-Store Home.pdf C-05..C-08, Epic E) ⚠ open question OI-25 ⚠ open question OI-26

## 7. Non-Functional
- Changes to the shared structure editor must be assessed against both hosts; the target is zero host-specific defects (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.19)
- Destructive roster actions are confirmation-gated and tracked (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.19)
- Number-locking must cover every entry surface. A non-trivial rate of duplicate errors at submit means some surface is bypassing the picker (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.19)
- No latency, volume or audit targets are stated ⚠ open question OI-27

## 8. Acceptance Criteria
- AC-1: Given a League store, when My Team(s) opens, then it shows the stats header (divisions · teams · players), the canvas, the standalone-teams list, and New Team / New Division (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.7)
- AC-2: Given a Single-Team store, then no division tier or grouping UI shows, and the tab opens on the team's player table (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.7)
- AC-3: Given a division is deleted, then its teams become standalone with their rosters and players intact (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8)
- AC-4: Given the creation host, then branding, team code, Epic 6 affordances and the Roster Bank are not rendered, and the detail panel has no segmented tabs (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8, p.15)
- AC-5: Given a division or team with no effective manager, then its Order Fulfillment strip shows "Not assigned" with a Manage link (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.8)
- AC-6: Given the add row opens, then it contains every schema field (address only on Direct-to-Home), and only First and Last Name are required (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- AC-7: Given a valid record, when the green check is confirmed, then the record appears in the table instantly (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- AC-8: Given the Upload Roster modal opens, then it offers a drag-drop/browse zone and a "Download CSV template" link (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- AC-9: Given an uploaded file, then errors are reported per row and cell (e.g. "Row 12: Missing last name"), invalid rows are skipped, valid rows import, and a preview with valid/skipped counts is shown before confirm (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- AC-10: Given a CSV row with no email or phone, then it imports. Given a malformed email, then the row is skipped (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- AC-11: Given a duplicate number in the CSV, then it is an error only if the target roster disallows duplicates (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9, p.22)
- AC-12: Given Edit from the row menu, then a side drawer opens pre-populated, with the player's own number selectable (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.9)
- AC-13: Given a single delete, then the player is removed without confirmation. Given a bulk delete, then a confirmation modal shows first. In both cases the numbers become selectable immediately (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- AC-14: Given Clear Roster, then the modal states the committed-player count, that the roster is kept, and whether captures are pending. On confirm, the toast reads "Roster cleared — n players removed". The button is disabled when the roster is empty (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- AC-15: Given Delete Roster on a League store, then on confirm the team survives with no roster, the view returns to team detail, and the toast reads 'Roster "name" deleted'. Delete Roster is not offered on a Single-Team store (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- AC-16: Given Clear or Delete Roster, then pending captures and product assignments for that team are unchanged (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- AC-17: Given a bulk Share Team Store Link, then recipients are prefilled from contact + associated emails, no-email players are listed copy-only, and if no selected player has an email, no send is offered (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- AC-18: Given a player with no contact channel, then Send Reminder is disabled with a reason stated (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.10)
- AC-19: Given a player with orders, when Jersey Number or Name on Jersey is edited, then it saves with no block, modal or warning, and the order line is unchanged (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11)
- AC-20: Given ordered personalization differs from the roster, then the Team Gear column and gear drawer show both values (e.g. "ordered as #12 · roster says #7") (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11)
- AC-21: Given the order is pending, then the divergence line shows a single per-line "update the order to #N" action. Given the order is submitted, then no action shows, and a server call to update is refused (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11, p.20)
- AC-22: Given divergence exists, then submission, the launch gate, Home cards and team-gear completion are all unaffected (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.11)
- AC-23: Given a Direct-to-Home store, then the add/edit form has an address input and the table has a Player Address column. For any other fulfillment model, neither shows (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12)
- AC-24: Given an address is entered, when the record is saved, then "Verifying address with map service…" shows during the check, and a failure offers retry or leave blank (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12)
- AC-25: Given a blank address on an existing record, when the parent enters one at checkout, then it writes back to the roster with a "From checkout" badge. Blank addresses show "Awaiting checkout" (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12)
- AC-26: Given an order with no matching player record, then a Roster Bank capture is created and no write-back happens (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.12)
- AC-27: Given a roster that disallows duplicates, then numbers held by committed players or pending captures are disabled on the add row, edit drawer, PDP and checkout (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13)
- AC-28: Given number entry, then the full number range is presented as a grid with taken numbers greyed out (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13)
- AC-29: Given CSV or direct entry of a taken number on a no-duplicates roster, then "Number [X] is already assigned to [Player Name]" fires (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13)
- AC-30: Given a roster that allows duplicates, then all numbers are selectable, no conflict error fires, and an in-use number is labelled "duplicate" (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13-14)
- AC-31: Given rosters disagree with the league default, then the Settings control shows a mixed state, and each roster view states its policy in force (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.13)
- AC-32: Given a roster with shared numbers, when duplicates are turned off, then the setting saves with no sweep or prompt, and no record, number or order changes (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.14)
- AC-33: Given grandfathered duplicates, then the roster view shows "duplicates off · N players share #X…" with the affected rows identifiable, and no third player can take #X (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.14)
- AC-34: Given one of the sharing players is deleted, moved or renumbered, or duplicates are turned back on, then the exception clears with no action (TS-Roster Management.pdf p.14)
