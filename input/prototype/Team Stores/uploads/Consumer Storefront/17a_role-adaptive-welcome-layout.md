# Role-Adaptive Welcome & Layout

### Executive Summary & Problem Statement

#### The Problem

Players/parents and fans have completely different needs; one layout serves neither well.

#### The Solution

A parent landing on required gear first; a fan landing on fan merchandise; a returning buyer.

### User persona & use cases

**Who is this for?** All buyers, branched by role.

**Scenarios encountered** Updating a shipping address; turning off marketing messages; reviewing linked players.

‌

### Functional Requirements & Core Workflows

#### User Stories

* As a buyer, I want a home tailored to my role, so that the most relevant gear is front and center.

#### Acceptance Criteria

* Given I am a player/parent, when home renders, then required/supplied team gear leads.
* Given I am a fan, when home renders, then fan gear leads and no required/supplied items appear.

‌

### Metrics & Success Criteria

* Role-appropriate first-action rate
* Bounce rate from home

---

‌

### Dependencies & Technical Constraints

**Dependency & Technical Constraint**

Requires role/entitlement from the account-roster link and role-based catalog visibility. Shared services.
