# Clarification Questions — Store Home (Home Tab)
Sources: Store Workspace/Store Home (Home Tab).md, cross-checked against the other 54 pages in Team-Store-Confluence/ (mainly Tabbed Store Dashboard, Launch & Status Controls, Store Status & Lifecycle, Order Management, Store Settings, Roster Management, Products Tab, System Modals & Toasts, Product Categories, Step 1 — Basics, Step 3 — Review & Deploy) · Total: 46 (P1 5 · P2 21 · P3 20)

Terms used below: **Home** is the new first tab in a store's workspace. A **card** is one item in Home's "Needs your attention" list. Each card type has an ID (C-01, C-02 …) and a **tier**: **Blocking** (red, something is stopped), **Action** (blue, work is waiting) or **Informational** (grey, good to know). The **rep** is the store owner using the workspace.
