# Prototype Answers — Team Stores Landing Page
Prototype: input/prototype/Team Stores (static HTML pages plus React JSX, no backend; source read only, nothing run) · Questions: 43 · Answered 5 · Partial 21 · Contradicts 3 · Not in prototype 14

How to read the evidence column:
- `Landing Page.dc.html` = `input/prototype/Team Stores/Landing Page.dc.html` (the splash page and all its sign-in pop-ups).
- `workspace-app.jsx` = `input/prototype/Team Stores/workspace-app.jsx` (the workspace behind `Workspace.dc.html`).
- `workspace-app-integrated.jsx` = `input/prototype/Team Stores/workspace-app-integrated.jsx` (the Marucci copy of the workspace, behind `Marucci Workspace.dc.html`). It has the same sign-in screens as the main copy, at line numbers about 15–25 higher. The one difference that matters here: the Marucci copy gives the rep two brands (PROLOOK and Victus) instead of one.
- The prototype has a "Tweaks" panel: a designer's control panel for switching between demo situations (for example "Dealership has Team Stores access" on/off). Answers that depend on a switch say so.
- "qx7" is Prolook's dealership database. "Customizer" is a brand's own design website.
