## Still open for requirement owners
- Q-1: The prototype counts all products, hidden ones included (L&S rule). Decide whether to keep that and change Store Home C-09/C-16, or switch to visible-only.
- Q-2: Decide which items count (hidden items, Incomplete bundles), whether confirm re-checks the gate, and what happens to an Open store whose last product is removed. None of this is in the prototype.
- Q-3: The prototype sends no launch email and uses share link + team codes instead. Decide whether the launch email exists at all, and if so, who gets it and what the modal says.
- Q-4: The prototype duplicates the store into a new Draft ("Use as Template") and saves no template. Decide between duplicate and saved template, and confirm what is copied and who can see it.
- Q-5: Launch, Preview and Template have no role checks in the prototype. Decide which roles may launch and create templates, and define "league director".
- Q-6: The prototype applies no post-launch lock and gives no warning. Define the lock list and the modal copy.
- Q-7: Define launch failure handling, idempotency, concurrent launches and async email/milestone (backend).
- Q-8: Decide whether a store can launch after its Closes date and what launching before Opens means. The prototype ignores dates at launch.
- Q-9: The prototype offers "Use as Template" only on store cards, in every state including Draft and Archived. Decide the entry point(s) and the per-state availability.
- Q-10: Decide new tab vs same tab for Preview, and what a rep sees for Closed and Draft stores. The prototype shows Preview in Draft and Closed with no preview bypass.
- Q-12: Confirm that Closed is a separate persisted state, and remove "technically equivalent to a draft". The prototype's `live: false` makes Closed cards read "Draft".
- Q-16: Define the `store.launched` event contract, and decide whether the rep sees launch as a completed milestone (the prototype's unmounted checklist does show one).
- Q-17: Rename "Active Stores" and the "On Hold" labels to Open / Closed, or keep "Active Stores" as a metric name only.
- Q-18: Specify the header for each state. The prototype header has no status chip, URL or store menu, and shows Preview Store in Draft. Also settle the chip colours: Closed is red on the Store status card, On Hold is amber on cards, and the spec suggests amber.
