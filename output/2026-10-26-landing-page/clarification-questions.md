# Clarification Questions — Team Stores Landing Page
Sources: input/requirements/Team-Store-Confluence/Global Navigation/Team Stores Landing Page.md (Confluence, last modified 2026-10-13, pulled 2026-10-26), cross-checked against the other 54 .md files in input/requirements/Team-Store-Confluence/ (mainly Account Page, Workspace, Access - Authentication, Team Stores Platform Overview, Store Settings, Customize New Product, Products Tab, System Emails, System Modals & Toasts) · Total: 43 (P1 4 · P2 25 · P3 14)

How to read this. A few terms come up again and again:
- **Customizer**: a brand's own online design tool, where a rep may already have an account (one per brand).
- **qx7**: Prolook's internal database that records which dealership a rep belongs to (matched by email) and which brands that dealership may sell.
- **Native account**: a Team Stores account that the rep creates directly with an email and password, without a Customizer.
- **Primary brand**: the one brand Team Stores treats as the rep's main brand, for example the brand the "Customizer" shortcut in the account menu opens.
- **Brand connection**: Team Stores' record that a rep's Customizer account for a brand is linked to them, so they can add that brand's products.
- **Access gate**: the check that keeps a rep out of the Team Stores tools until Prolook approves their business.
- **Splash page**: the first page a signed-out visitor sees.
- **OI-n**: an open item raised in implementation-summary.md. Each one is answered by the question that lists it.
