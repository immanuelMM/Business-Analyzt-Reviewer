How to read this: card numbers such as C-03 are the cards in the Home tab's "Needs your attention" list. Each card has a colour group: Blocking (red, someone is already stopped), Action (blue, rep work is waiting) or Informational (grey, good to know). OI numbers point to open items in implementation-summary.md.
