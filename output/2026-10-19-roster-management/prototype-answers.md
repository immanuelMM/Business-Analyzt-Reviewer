# Prototype Answers — Roster Management (My Team(s) Tab)
Prototype: input/prototype/Team Stores/ (React 18 JSX via Babel standalone in .dc.html hosts; no build, no backend; state in React state, `window` globals and `localStorage`) · Questions: 44 · Answered 11 · Partial 17 · Contradicts 9 · Not in prototype 7

Notes on sources:
- Workspace evidence is from `input/prototype/Team Stores/workspace-app-integrated.jsx` (abbreviated `WAI.jsx` below; full path = `input/prototype/Team Stores/workspace-app-integrated.jsx`). Per the prototype's own project rule, `workspace-app.jsx` is kept identical.
- Storefront evidence comes from `Storefront.html:382`. That line is a built bundle: a gzipped, base64-encoded copy of `_export-storefront-app.jsx`. It was decoded for reading only, and the cited "dL" numbers are lines in the decoded source. Storefront rows are therefore Confidence Low at best.
- In this prototype a **team and its roster are one object**. `org.groups[].rosters[]` are the teams of a division, and `org.loose[]` are the standalone teams. Each one carries a `players` count and opens a player table, so "roster" in the code usually means "team".
