# Customizer & Picker Page

The sport-and-category picker that begins a new product customization and hands off to the external design tool.

## Sport / Category Picker & Hand-off

### Executive Summary & Problem Statement

#### The Problem

Starting a custom product from a blank slate is overwhelming; reps need a guided entry by sport and category.

#### The Solution

A grid of sport/category cards (baseball, basketball, football, soccer, apparel, accessories, and gendered variants); selecting a card hands off to the product customizer flow.

### User persona & use cases

**Who is this for?** Reps creating and adding new custom products.

**Scenarios encountered** Beginning a new uniform

### Functional Requirements & Core Workflows

#### User Stories

* As a rep, I want to pick a sport/category to start customizing, so that I enter the right design template quickly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

* Given I open the Customizer, when I select a sport/category card, then I am handed off to the customizer flow for that template.

### Metrics & Success Criteria

* Picker→customizer hand-off completion
* Time to start a new product

---

### Dependencies & Technical Constraints

**Dependency & Technical Constraint**

Requires integration with the external "Customizer 3.0" design tool (referenced but outside these files) and a catalog of sport/category templates. Third-party / internal design-tool integration.
