Turn 17 — Price settings: horizontal rhythm on wide screens

Today the grid repeats its header per size and pins Dealer profit / Fundraising to fixed-width tracks while Retail floats to the far edge — on a wide card that leaves two voids and the eye has to jump across them. All four shown at 1240px. 17a one header, content-sized table capped in width, 17b the math as a row (base + profit + fundraising = retail) on equal tracks, 17c one card per size, side by side, 17d transposed — sizes become the columns.
17ACompact table, capped width

A real table: one header row, sizes as rows, every column sized to its content and Retail right-aligned immediately after Fundraising. The table itself stops at 820px, so a wider card adds whitespace to the right instead of stretching the gaps. Fundraising toggle and the customer-price footer stay full width beneath.
