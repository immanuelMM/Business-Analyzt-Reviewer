FAQ

Sign-in, brands & onboarding

I'm already signed into my Customizer — do I log in again?+

No. Team Stores recognizes you automatically and signs you in with the same Customizer credentials — no second login. It opens in a new browser tab so your Customizer work stays exactly where you left it, and routes you straight to your Overview.

The platform looks like my brand — how does that work?+

Team Stores is brand-agnostic. When you sign in from a Customizer, the platform carries that Customizer's logo through your dashboard. If you're not signed in, you'll see the default PROLOOK branding until you choose a Customizer to continue.

I sell multiple brands — can I add products from all of them?+

Yes. If you're authorized to sell other brands under the same email, you're automatically signed into those brand Customizers and can add their products to any store you build.

My dealership is new to Team Stores — what happens first?+

You'll complete a short business-details form — legal entity, tax and banking info. We verify it instantly through Stripe, so there's no waiting period: once it's confirmed you go straight into your dashboard.

What if I'm not logged in when I click Team Stores?+

You'll be asked to choose your Customizer, then sign in with your existing Customizer credentials. From there you continue straight to Overview — or, if your dealership is new, to the quick business-details form.
